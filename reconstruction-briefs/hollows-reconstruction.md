# Hollows - Canon-Constrained Reconstruction Brief

## 1. What is explicitly known
- Simple and square, with stained glass windows depicting Teccam speaking at the mouth of his cave
- Contains admissions theatre: empty theatre-like room with raised crescent-shaped masters' table in dark room lit mainly around the table
- Has back entrance with locks, allowing internal partitioning
- Elodin has an office in Hollows, though unused
- Contains Elodin's lecture hall: huge empty lecture hall entered from hallway above, students walk down stairs to front row, large slate at front
- Reached from Archives by going "down, then left"; identifiable by colored windows and two big maples outside

## 2. What is strongly implied
- Ceremonial and administrative functions with theatrical staging
- Multiple access points and circulation paths
- Contains at least one master's office space
- Positioned lower or farther down grade from Archives
- Formal admissions process with elevated authority

## 3. What is weakly inferred
- Square stone building with notable stained glass façade
- Interior theatre space designed to reinforce authority
- Administrative offices for faculty members
- Complex access patterns with public and private routes

## 4. What must not be contradicted
- No complex or ornate exterior beyond stained glass
- No single public entrance only
- No lack of internal partitioning
- No absence of master's offices
- No disconnection from Archives access route

## 5. Likely building function and circulation
Hollows serves as admissions and ceremonial building with theatrical staging for authority reinforcement, functioning as both administrative center and formal admissions space. Circulation includes both public ceremonial routes and private access paths, with multiple entry points and internal divisions.

## 6. Likely entrances, stairs, and room zones
- Main entrance from Archives approach ("down, then left")
- Back entrance with lockable access
- Internal routes to admissions theatre and offices
- Lecture hall entered from upper hallway level
- Master's office spaces
- Ceremonial theatre with raised staging

## 7. Unknowns that must remain unknown
- Exact number of master's offices
- Complete internal layout
- Specific stained glass window locations
- Exact relationship to Masters' Hall
- Full extent of back entrance access
- Lecture hall exact dimensions

## 8. Final reconstruction summary
A simple square stone building with stained glass windows depicting Teccam, featuring a ceremonial admissions theatre with raised masters' table, multiple access points including a lockable back entrance, master's offices, and a large lecture hall entered from above. The building serves formal admissions functions with theatrical authority staging and complex internal circulation patterns.
