# The Mains - Canon-Constrained Reconstruction Brief

- Oldest University building, grown slowly in all directions engulfing smaller buildings and courtyards
- Maze-like circulation with odd turns, dead ends, rambling routes, and shortcut paths through workrooms and lecture halls

- Contains theatre-style lecture rooms with tiered semicircular seating around raised stages and stone worktables
- Roof accessible via drainpipes in sheltered box alley
- Roof-to-courtyard access via apple tree descent

Circulation is intentionally inefficient with multiple route options, shortcuts, and dead ends, reflecting organic historical development rather than planned design.
