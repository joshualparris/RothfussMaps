# Haven - Canon-Constrained Reconstruction Brief

## 1. What is explicitly known
- Haven stands outside the dense campus core and is approached from the north.
- It is huge, formal, and manor-like rather than dungeon-like.
- It has a red tile roof, high windows, arched doorways, pillars, fountains, flowers, and hedges.
- A nearly ten-foot wrought-iron fence encloses it and is effectively unclimbable.
- The entryway is grand, with stained glass and vaulted ceilings.
- Inside are a wide marble staircase and long white hallways lined with wooden doors.
- It houses hundreds of patients, with room for hundreds more.
- Alder Whin's room is daylit but padded for safety and muffled sound.
- Elodin's former cell sits in a different wing and uses copper-heavy high-containment design.

## 2. What is strongly implied
- Haven is a very large institution with multiple wings or zones.
- Security level changes as one moves inward.
- The building combines public-facing dignity with hidden coercive architecture.
- Different patient types are separated spatially.

## 3. What is weakly inferred
- Intake, guard, and administrative functions are present near the formal entrance.
- Ordinary wards, specialized safety rooms, and extreme-containment rooms are not mixed casually together.
- The grounds are maintained and deliberate, not overgrown.

## 4. What must not be contradicted
- Do not turn Haven into Masters' offices or faculty governance space.
- Do not depict Haven as a tiny annex or a crude prison.
- Do not omit the fence or formal grounds.
- Do not make all patient rooms identical.

## 5. Likely building function and circulation
Haven functions as a large asylum-compound with a dignified public face and increasingly controlled interior zones. Circulation likely progresses from formal arrival spaces to ordinary wards and then to more secure and specialized wings.

## 6. Likely entrances, stairs, and room zones
- Main gate through fenced grounds.
- Formal entrance hall with stained glass and vaulted ceiling.
- Central stair or stair hall.
- Long ward corridors.
- Ordinary rooms.
- Padded safety rooms.
- Separate high-containment wing or chamber set.

## 7. Unknowns that must remain unknown
- Exact wing count.
- Exact floor count.
- Exact placement of Whin's room and Elodin's former cell.
- Exact arrangement of staff spaces.

## 8. Final reconstruction summary
Haven is a large, formal, heavily secured institutional manor north of the University core, distinguished by its fence, landscaped grounds, grand entry, marble stair, and long corridor logic. Its interior should support multiple layers of care and containment, from ordinary wards to padded rooms to copper-based high-security chambers.
