# The Mews - Building Brief

## Building Identity
Radial student residence built around a central hub, with status-based assignments and one important canon contradiction that must be preserved rather than ignored.

## Locked Canon
- Circular central hub with eight wings radiating outward like a compass rose.
- Grey, many-windowed, and externally described as three stories tall.
- Ground floor bathing facilities exist and matter to room assignment logic.
- Non-Arcanum bunks are on the fourth floor of the east wing and are farthest from the baths.
- Arcanum students are housed in the west wing.
- The Mess lies across a lawn to the south.

## Frozen Design Decision
- Reconcile the "three stories tall" exterior with the "fourth floor of the east wing" interior by treating the Mews as three dominant exterior stories plus at least one partial upper level, attic-like dormer level, or split-level upper accommodation in the east wing. Mark this as reconstructed whenever needed.

## Major Zones
- Central hub for circulation and vertical movement.
- Eight outward wings with unequal or at least differently assigned residential use.
- Ground-floor bathing suite or suites.
- Residential upper floors, with east and west wing roles partially known.
- South-facing relationship to the Mess across open lawn.

## Hard Bans
- No rectangular dormitory block.
- No identical wing assignments everywhere.
- No baths moved upstairs.
- No claim that all four floors are equally obvious from the exterior.
- No reassignment of the known west-wing and east-wing roles.

## Prompt-Ready Summary
Grey radial dormitory organized around a circular hub with eight wings, built for circulation and status-based student sorting rather than elegant symmetry. Preserve the known contradiction by showing a building that reads as three stories from the outside but can still support a partial fourth-level east-wing bunk zone above the main massing. Keep baths on the ground floor and the Mess across lawn to the south.
