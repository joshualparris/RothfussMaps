# University Evidence Summary v1

This is a first-pass synthesis of the strongest spatial evidence logged in [university-evidence-ledger-v1.csv](./university-evidence-ledger-v1.csv).

## Locked High-Value Truths

- The University is west of Imre, with Stonebridge crossing the Omethi between them.
- The Archives are a dominant monolithic block: square, grey, windowless, and five stories tall.
- The Archives have a two-stage entrance and a branching antechamber with TOMES, STACKS, and SCRIVS ONLY access.
- The Mains are not symmetrical. They are old, accreted, maze-like, and contain awkward absorbed courtyards.
- The Mews are radial, not rectangular. East and west wings matter, and floor assignments matter.
- The Medica sits on the other side of the Archives and reads as a large irregular medical-hospital building.
- The Artificery/Fishery is a real industrial complex with multiple trades, not a single forge room.
- Haven lies off the northern edge of the University on a forest road and is fenced, manor-like, and much larger than a simple ward.
- The Underthing matters. It is not optional lore dressing if you want a serious University layout.

## Strong Building Notes

- `the Archives`
  Exterior is unusually well locked by canon.
  Interior is not a single library hall. It includes Tomes, the Stacks, the Scriptorium, Sorting Hall, acquisitions office, and more.
- `the Mains`
  Best treated as a grown-over academic labyrinth with absorbed rooms and trapped courtyards.
  A clean symmetric floor plan would be a canon miss.
- `the Mews`
  Best treated as a hub-and-spoke dormitory complex.
  East wing, west wing, fourth-floor bunks, and ground-floor baths all matter.
- `the Fishery / Artificery`
  Best treated as a workshop complex with storage, multiple craft zones, catwalks, and hot industrial infrastructure.
- `Haven`
  Best treated as a separate secured institution on the campus edge rather than a room or annex inside the main academic cluster.

## Important Gaps Still Open

- Exact global orientation of Mains, Mews, Fishery, Masters' Hall, and Crucible relative to one another is still underdetermined.
- Hollows is only strongly fixed relative to the Archives, not to the whole campus.
- The pennant courtyard is identified by use and appearance, but not yet fully anchored in the campus map.
- The exact footprint relationship between Artificery and Fishery is still best handled as "same complex / same commonly named place" until more evidence is logged.
- The full surface access map of the Underthing is still incomplete.
