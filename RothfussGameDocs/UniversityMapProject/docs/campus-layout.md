# University Campus Layout Freeze (`campus_v1`)

Source of truth for this freeze: `university-evidence-ledger-v3.csv`.

## Locked Canon
- The University lies west of Imre and is reached by crossing Stonebridge over the Omethi canyon.
- The built environment around the University is town-sized, orderly, and service-oriented rather than a tiny isolated cloister.
- The campus has about fifteen architecturally varied buildings and supports over a thousand students.
- Admissions is a major outdoor logistical event lasting eleven days, with lines and milling crowds in a large open square or courtyard.
- The Archives are the strongest exterior anchor: a five-story square grey windowless monolith visible from Stonebridge.
- Mains is the oldest and most spatially chaotic academic core: accreted, sprawling, and full of absorbed spaces.
- Hollows is a small square stained-glass building near the Archives and is reached from the Archives by going down and then left.
- The Medica is a large irregular building and is encountered opposite or beyond the Archives from one known approach.
- The Fishery or Artificery is an industrial complex, not a single room, with a south exit toward a courtyard and low windows on its east wall.
- The Mews are radial and many-windowed, with a central hub and eight wings; the Mess lies across a lawn to the south.
- Haven is outside the dense academic core to the north, approached by road, fenced, and much more like a formal manor-institution than a prison block.
- The Underthing is a large multi-zone hidden network connected to the Archives and accessible from surface grates.

## Working Campus Logic
- Stonebridge is the eastern chokepoint and the Archives are the first major visual landmark on the University side.
- The core campus should be treated as a loose academic cluster with roads, lawns, gardens, and service circulation between buildings rather than wall-to-wall density.
- Mains functions as the central academic labyrinth.
- Archives and Hollows form the most strongly evidenced eastern administrative or ceremonial cluster.
- Medica and Fishery belong on the productive or service-heavy side of campus rather than inside the ceremonial core.
- Mews and Mess belong together in the residential belt, with Mess south of Mews across open lawn.
- Haven should stay isolated from the student core and read as its own secured compound at the north edge.

## Frozen Design Decisions
- Treat `House of the Wind`, `Pennant Square`, and the whipping courtyard as one working central square until later evidence forces a split. Keep the alternate naming in notes.
- Treat `Belows` and earlier `Billows` as the same deep wind-tunnel Underthing zone, with spelling uncertainty preserved in notes.
- Reconcile the Mews height tension by treating the building as three full exterior stories with at least one partial upper level or attic-like fourth-floor accommodation in the east wing.
- Keep project north as map-up for consistency, but treat most campus-wide compass placement outside the Imre/Stonebridge/University axis as reconstructed rather than proven.

## Adjacency Spine
- `Imre` -> `Stonebridge` -> University approach.
- `Stonebridge` -> sightline to `the Archives`.
- `the Archives` -> near `Hollows`; opposite or beyond `the Medica` from one courtyard approach; connected to `the Underthing`.
- `Hollows` -> near `the Archives`; contains the admissions theatre and Elodin's office.
- `the Mews` -> `the Mess` across lawn to the south.
- `the Fishery` -> south-facing exit toward a courtyard; likely tied to the working-yard side of campus.
- `Mains roof` -> courtyard edge via apple tree.
- `Haven` -> north road, outside dense campus core.

## Open Questions
- Exact compass placement of Mains, Mews, Fishery, Crucible, Ledgers and Lists, and Masters' Hall remains partly reconstructed.
- The exact relation between House of the Wind, Pennant Square, and Questioning Hall is still a naming problem, not a solved canon fact.
- The exact siting of Masters' Hall and the Bursar relative to Hollows and the central square is still incomplete.
- The number and placement of Underthing access points beyond the Archives are still underdetermined.

## Prompt-Ready Campus Brief
Large medieval university west of Imre across Stonebridge, embedded in an orderly town-sized settlement with roads, lawns, gardens, and specialist shops. Use the Archives as the eastern visual monolith, Mains as the sprawling central labyrinth, Hollows as the nearby ceremonial stained-glass building, Medica and Fishery as large working institutions, Mews and Mess as the residential cluster, and Haven as a separate northern secured manor-compound. Keep the central cobblestone gathering square large enough for admissions queues and public punishments, and preserve uncertainty where canon does not lock exact compass placement.
