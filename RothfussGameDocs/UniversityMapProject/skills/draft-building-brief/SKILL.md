# Draft Building Brief

Use this workflow after evidence has been gathered and before floor JSON is produced.

## Purpose

Turn evidence into a restrained building brief that separates:

- locked canon
- strong implication
- clearly marked reconstruction

## Required Sections

- Building identity
- Locked canon
- Frozen design decisions
- Major zones
- Hard bans
- Open uncertainties
- Prompt-ready summary

## Rules

- Do not include decorative invention.
- Keep institutional logic practical.
- Use uncertainty notes instead of fake precision.
- Do not freeze a brief until the review gate is approved.

## Review Gate

`building_brief_frozen`
