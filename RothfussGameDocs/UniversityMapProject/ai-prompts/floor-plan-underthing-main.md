Create a strictly top-down floor plan for a representative main level of the Underthing beneath the University from Patrick Rothfuss's *Kingkiller Chronicle* as a canon-constrained reconstruction.

## FLOOR IDENTITY
This is not the whole Underthing. It is one navigable level or segment that demonstrates the Underthing's tunnel variety, hidden access logic, and named zones.

## LOCKED CANON
- Entry can occur through an iron drainage grate.
- A proper tunnel and a belly-crawl route lead toward hidden Archives access.
- Named areas include Nodway, Vaults, Woods, Cricklet, and Belows or Billows.
- One zone is heated by exposed steam pipes.
- Woods has support beams.
- Cricklet is long, low, and watery.
- Vaults is narrow and cracked across the floor.

## STRONG IMPLICATIONS
- The plan should mix broad tunnels, cramped passages, rooms, and hazardous floor conditions.
- Different branches should feel like different ages or purposes of underground space.
- The main level should lead toward deeper territory rather than pretending to contain the whole system.

- Start with one clear entry chamber or tunnel from the iron grate.
- Build a main route that branches into at least two distinct zone types.
- Place Vaults as a narrow corridor with visible floor cracks.
- Place Cricklet as a long low side room or branch with water along one wall.
- Put Woods in a larger ruined chamber sequence with timber supports.
- Use a hotter service branch for exposed steam pipes.
- Let one cramped crawl or ventilation route angle toward the Archives.
- Show descent or transition toward deeper Belows/Billows rather than flattening the whole deep maze into the same easy level.

## FIXED ELEMENTS AND FURNITURE

- Fixed elements are grates, support beams, cracks, pipes, ledges, crawl gaps, and water channels.
- If a found object or storage niche is shown, keep it rare and reconstructed.
