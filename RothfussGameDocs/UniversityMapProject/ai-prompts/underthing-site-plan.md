Create a strictly top-down, orthographic subterranean site plan of the Underthing beneath the University from Patrick Rothfuss's *Kingkiller Chronicle*.

This is a canon-constrained reconstruction, not fantasy concept art.

## OUTPUT GOAL
Produce a single black-and-white underground site diagram showing:
- one representative surface-near entry through an iron drainage grate
- the major tunnel network beneath the University
- the Archives connection by ventilation or crawl-access logic
- named zones such as Nodway, Vaults, Woods, Cricklet, and Belows or Billows
- only ghosted surface references where needed for orientation

## LOCKED CANON
- Auri leads Kvothe into the Underthing through an iron drainage grate.
- The Underthing is extensive and multi-zone, explored over many nights.
- Named routes include Nodway, Vaults, and Billows or Belows.
- One zone is heated by exposed steam pipes.
- A proper tunnel and a long belly-crawl route give hidden access to the Archives through ventilation logic.
- The Woods has crumbling halls and timber support beams.
- Cricklet is a long low room with water down one wall.
- Vaults is a narrow hallway crossed by three deep cracks.
- Belows or Billows is a deep windy maze of tunnels.

## STRONG IMPLICATIONS
- The Underthing is not a neat sewer grid.
- Tunnel widths, room shapes, and floor conditions should vary sharply.
- Some routes are ordinary walking tunnels, others are crawls, drops, or deeper descents.

## OPENING AND MASSING DISCIPLINE
- Show the surface grate clearly as one discrete entry, not a broad staircase.
- Use varying tunnel widths, occasional chambers, and abrupt route changes.
- Place the Archives connection on a service or ventilation branch, not as a grand doorway.
- Let the steam-pipe zone read as a hotter service corridor.
- Keep deeper Billows or Belows below or beyond the main navigable level, reached by descent rather than already flattened into the same plane.

## HARD BANS
- no simple sewer ring map
- no large surface building footprint treated as the Underthing itself
- no disconnection from the Archives
- no uniform corridor widths everywhere

## LABELS TO INCLUDE
- `iron grate entry`
- `Nodway`
- `Vaults`
- `Woods`
- `Cricklet`
- `Belows/Billows`
- `Archives access` or equivalent
- `steam pipes` if shown

## UNCERTAINTY HANDLING
If a link between zones is uncertain:
- keep it lightly drawn
- label it reconstructed
- preserve the network feeling without pretending to complete the whole underground map

## CONSISTENCY REQUIREMENTS
- Keep north aligned with the master campus map where surface references appear.
- Keep scale consistent with the approved building shell.
- Preserve previously approved entrances, corridors, stairs, and adjacent rooms.
- Do not contradict earlier approved plans.
- If a new detail would force a contradiction, reject the detail rather than improvising.
