Create a strictly top-down floor plan for the Mess, Ground Floor, from Patrick Rothfuss's *Kingkiller Chronicle* as a canon-constrained reconstruction.

## FLOOR IDENTITY
This floor should be dominated by one large communal dining room inside a long, low-roofed building across the lawn from the Mews.

## LOCKED CANON
- The Mess is across the lawn from the Mews.
- The building is long and low-roofed.
- The dining room seats roughly two hundred students.
- The room uses large plank tables.

## STRONG IMPLICATIONS
- The main hall should occupy most of the footprint.
- Kitchen, pantry, and service support are strongly implied but not precisely described.
- The public approach should come from the lawn/Mews side.

## PLACEMENT DISCIPLINE
- Keep the footprint elongated.
- Place the main entrance on the side most likely to face the lawn and Mews.
- Let a vestibule or short buffer lead into the main hall rather than placing the door directly at the end of a table row.
- Put the kitchen and service rooms on one side or one end of the building so the public dining room remains legible.
- Use windows only on true exterior walls, most likely along the long sides of the hall.
- Keep one service door separate from the main entrance if needed, and label it reconstructed if not clearly canon.

## FIXED ELEMENTS AND FURNITURE
- Fill the dining hall with rows of large plank tables and benches.
- Keep aisles wide enough for serving and circulation.
- Put serving counter, hatch, or pass-through near the kitchen edge rather than inventing multiple food stations.
- Pantry, scullery, or storage may be modest and functional.

## HARD BANS
- no cathedral refectory grandeur
- no tiny dining room inconsistent with the seating clue
- no kitchenless hall
- no breaking the lawn relation to the Mews
