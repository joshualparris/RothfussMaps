Create a top-down architectural site plan of the Mains and its immediate exterior context from the Kingkiller Chronicle as a canon-constrained reconstruction, not a fantasy invention.

Style:
clean black-and-white planning drawing
architectural site plan
north arrow
simple labels
no decorative fantasy art
no characters
no dramatic lighting
measured, restrained, scholarly presentation

Required constraints:
- Nearly an acre and a half in footprint.
- Must look cobbled together from mismatched pieces.
- Should read as a building that swallowed older buildings and courtyards.
- Include at least one sheltered exterior alley or box with drainpipes that plausibly gives roof access.
- The exterior massing should support a roofline full of chimneys and mixed slate, clay, and tin.
- Immediate exterior context should allow for at least one courtyard edge reachable from roof by apple tree.

Hard bans:
- no symmetry
- no clean quadrangle block
- no singular unified facade
- no simple flat roof

Rules:
- Show only the Mains and the exterior context needed to explain its accreted shape.
- If a courtyard or annex is reconstructed, label it clearly.
- Use a plain map key with `canon`, `implied`, and `reconstructed`.
- Keep the plan believable as a building grown across centuries rather than laid out at once.

Consistency requirements:
- Keep north aligned with the master campus map.
- Keep scale consistent with the approved building shell.
- Preserve previously approved entrances, corridors, stairs, and adjacent rooms.
- Do not contradict earlier approved plans.
- If a new detail would force a contradiction, reject the detail rather than improvising.

## OPENING AND MASSING DISCIPLINE
- Decide which face is the real public approach before placing doors.
- Put the main entrance on the approach side justified by the canon brief rather than centering it by default.
- Use additional doors only when the evidence or reconstruction logic truly requires them.
- Place windows only on true exterior walls and only when the building shell allows them.
- Let the outer mass explain the interior function: large halls need large uninterrupted spans, dense residence needs repeated openings, and industrial work needs chimney or service logic.
- If a doorway, wing, yard, or window band is uncertain, choose one conservative option and label it `reconstructed` instead of multiplying possibilities.
