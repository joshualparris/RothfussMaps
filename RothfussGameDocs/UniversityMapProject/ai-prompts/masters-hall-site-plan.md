Create a strictly top-down, orthographic architectural site plan of Masters' Hall and its immediate exterior context from Patrick Rothfuss's *Kingkiller Chronicle*.

This is a canon-constrained reconstruction, not fantasy concept art.

## OUTPUT GOAL
Produce a single black-and-white site plan showing:
- the exterior footprint of Masters' Hall
- only enough grounds to explain approach, private lodging logic, and the relation of residential versus formal spaces
- a dignified but not over-monumental senior-academic building

## LOCKED CANON
- Masters' Hall is grey stone.
- It shows stained glass of Teccam speaking at the mouth of his cave.
- It contains living quarters for the masters, resident gillers, and guest rooms for visiting arcanists.
- It includes a large formal room with a familiar crescent-shaped table.
- That formal chamber is distinct from the raised admissions theatre in Hollows.

## STRONG IMPLICATIONS
- Masters' Hall is mixed residential and governance space, not a pure office block.
- The formal chamber should feel separated from ordinary private lodging.
- Exterior windows are plausible for living quarters, but the known formal chamber itself should not be treated as obviously windowed.

## OPENING AND MASSING DISCIPLINE
- Use a dignified, conservative stone mass rather than a dorm block or palace.
- Provide one principal entrance and, at most, a small reconstructed service entrance.
- Let residential wings or lodging ranges read as calmer and more windowed than the deeper formal chamber zone.
- If the formal chamber is expressed in the massing, keep it as an internal or quieter block, not a glazed public pavilion.

## HARD BANS
- no claim that Elodin's office is here
- no pure office-block interpretation
- no dungeon keep silhouette
- no replacement of lodging with meeting rooms only

## LABELS TO INCLUDE
- `Masters' Hall`
- `main entrance`
- `lodging range` if used
- `formal chamber` if it helps orientation
- `reconstructed service` if used

## UNCERTAINTY HANDLING
If the exact wing arrangement is uncertain:
- keep the plan simple
- mark secondary masses reconstructed
- preserve the mixed residential-governance identity

## CONSISTENCY REQUIREMENTS
- Keep north aligned with the master campus map.
- Keep scale consistent with the approved building shell.
- Preserve previously approved entrances, corridors, stairs, and adjacent rooms.
- Do not contradict earlier approved plans.
- If a new detail would force a contradiction, reject the detail rather than improvising.
