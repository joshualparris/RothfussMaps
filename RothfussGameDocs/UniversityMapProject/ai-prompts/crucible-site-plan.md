Create a strictly top-down, orthographic architectural site plan of the Crucible and its immediate exterior context from Patrick Rothfuss's *Kingkiller Chronicle*.

This is a canon-constrained reconstruction, not fantasy concept art.

## OUTPUT GOAL
Produce a single black-and-white site plan showing:
- the exterior footprint of the Crucible
- the chimney-heavy roof silhouette logic
- only the surrounding yard or path context needed to explain why it reads as a harsh chemical or alchemical work building

## LOCKED CANON
- The Crucible has countless bristling chimneys.
- It smells of ammonia, charred flowers, acid, and alcohol.
- It should read as a chemical-industrial University building.

## STRONG IMPLICATIONS
- The building belongs to the practical or experimental side of campus, not the ceremonial side.
- A large interior workroom or several heat-intensive rooms are likely.
- Venting, flues, and fire-safe masonry edges are strongly implied, but exact systems remain reconstructed.

## OPENING AND MASSING DISCIPLINE
- Keep the overall mass compact to moderate, but make the roofline and chimney distribution dense and unmistakable.
- Use one primary student or work entrance on the most practical side, not a ceremonial forecourt.
- If a second service door is shown, keep it small and reconstructed.
- Exterior windows, if used, should be sparse and practical because the building prioritizes chimneys, fumes, and work surfaces.
- Let one wall or roof sector read as the heavier hearth and flue side.

## HARD BANS
- no residence or dormitory massing
- no clean lecture-hall facade with no industrial signature
- no decorative tower composition
- no fake certainty about internal departments

## LABELS TO INCLUDE
- `the Crucible`
- `main entry`
- `chimney cluster`
- `reconstructed service` if used

## UNCERTAINTY HANDLING
If yard structures or external stores are uncertain:
- keep them minimal
- label them reconstructed
- do not fill the site with alchemy clutter for atmosphere alone

## CONSISTENCY REQUIREMENTS
- Keep north aligned with the master campus map.
- Keep scale consistent with the approved building shell.
- Preserve previously approved entrances, corridors, stairs, and adjacent rooms.
- Do not contradict earlier approved plans.
