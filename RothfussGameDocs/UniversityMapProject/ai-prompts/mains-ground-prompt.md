# AI Image Generation Prompt: The Mains - Ground-Level Overview Pack

Use this as a ground-level identity prompt after the architectural plan is frozen. This is not the authoritative shell prompt; it is the atmosphere and circulation pack for what it feels like to move through the lower Mains.

## Base Style Guidelines
- medieval academic stone architecture
- accreted, inconvenient circulation
- worn floors, lived-in academic traffic
- no clean master-plan symmetry
- technical readability over fantasy flourish

## Ground-Level Overview Prompt

Generate a canon-constrained visual overview of the ground-level Mains from Patrick Rothfuss's Kingkiller Chronicle.

Locked canon:
- Mains is sprawling, old, and accreted from mismatched pieces.
- Ground-level circulation should involve odd turns, dead ends, shortcuts, and routes through workrooms or lecture spaces.
- At least one isolated courtyard exists somewhere in the Mains fabric and is awkward to access.
- Some teaching spaces are theatre-like rather than flat classrooms.
- The building should feel like it grew over time rather than being laid out all at once.

Hard bans:
- no rectangular master shell
- no exact dimensions
- no exact entrance count
- no central rational hall that solves the whole plan

## Scene Set

### Crooked Hall
- worn stone passage with awkward turns
- mixed door sizes and inherited wall lines
- sense of older and newer pieces jammed together

### Workroom Shortcut
- room or passage that plausibly acts as both work space and circulation cut-through

### Isolated Courtyard Glimpse
- exterior pocket swallowed by the building fabric
- visible but not part of normal easy flow

### Lecture Theatre
- descending academic room
- tiered seating around a stage or demonstration area

Consistency requirements:
- Keep north aligned with the master campus map.
- Keep scale consistent with the approved building shell.
- Preserve previously approved entrances, corridors, stairs, and adjacent rooms.
- Do not contradict earlier approved plans.
