Tomes is the public, high-ceilinged reading room at the threshold of the Archives. It is formal, controlled, and better lit than the rest of the building, but still windowless.

- Access comes from the antechamber through double doors.
- Tomes is large, high-ceilinged, and well lit.
- It contains long tables.
- It houses core class texts and public reading materials.
- Requests are submitted at the desk in the threshold area.
- The Archives have no windows.
