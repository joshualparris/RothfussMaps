Create a strictly top-down, orthographic architectural site plan of the bursar approach sequence and its immediate exterior context from Patrick Rothfuss's *Kingkiller Chronicle*.

This is a canon-constrained reconstruction, not fantasy concept art.

## OUTPUT GOAL
Produce a single black-and-white site plan showing:
- the courtyard edge used in the known route
- the stone building entered after crossing the courtyard
- the entry door and hallway alignment
- the stair position that leads down toward the bursar
- only the immediate surroundings needed to explain that sequence

## LOCKED CANON
- The bursar is reached by crossing a courtyard.
- The route then enters another stone building.
- Inside is a hallway.
- From that hallway, a flight of stairs leads down to the bursar.

## STRONG IMPLICATIONS
- The bursar is not a fully exposed street-front office; it sits deeper in the complex.
- The surface-level entry should be modest and practical.
- The lower office should feel more private and record-driven than the courtyard outside.

## OPENING AND MASSING DISCIPLINE
- Show one clear doorway from the courtyard into the stone building.
- Put the hallway immediately beyond the entrance rather than opening into a large public chamber.
- Place the stair down on the hall route before the final office zone.
- If exterior windows are shown at all, keep them conservative and tied only to real exterior walls; the descent route matters more than facade decoration.
- Do not allow the office program to erase the route sequence of courtyard -> stone building -> hallway -> stairs.

## HARD BANS
- no site plan that omits the courtyard
- no site plan that omits the stone building
- no site plan that omits the hallway
- no site plan that omits the stairs down
- no grand civic-bank facade
