Create a strictly top-down room plan for a representative Stacks district in the Archives from Patrick Rothfuss's *Kingkiller Chronicle* as a canon-constrained reconstruction.

## ROOM IDENTITY
Treat this as one local piece of the Stacks-city rather than the entire Stacks. It should feel dark, controlled, and navigable only by lamp.

## LOCKED CANON
- The Stacks are darker and separate from Tomes.
- They are unlit and require hand lamps.
- They are cool, dry, and fresh-aired despite having no windows.
- They use stairwells, layered routes, and shelving-coordinate logic.
- A named southeast stairwell exists.
- Small stone rooms with heavy wooden doors occur within the Stacks.

## STRONG IMPLICATIONS
- The plan should show shelving lanes, branch routes, and at least one meaningful vertical anchor.
- This district may include one side room or access door, but the shelves remain dominant.
- The local geometry should feel more maze-like than Tomes.

## ROOM ENVELOPE AND PLACEMENT DISCIPLINE
- Use one or two approach routes from the wider Stacks circulation rather than a public lobby.
- Put the southeast stairwell or another strong stair anchor on one edge of the district.
- Arrange shelves to create lanes, corners, and partial sightline breaks.
- Include one heavy-doored side room or enclosed chamber if useful, but keep it secondary.
- Do not place windows on any wall.
- Keep lamp use implicit in layout and labels rather than inventing bright fixtures everywhere.

## FIXED ELEMENTS AND FURNITURE
- Shelving ranges are the dominant fixed elements.
- Include stair, heavy door, and perhaps one small work table or sorting surface only if conservative.
- Keep loose furniture to an absolute minimum.

## LIGHT AND MATERIALS
- The room should read as stone, timber shelving, darkness, and dry air.
- Lighting logic is hand-lamp scale, not room-flooding illumination.

## HARD BANS
- no windows
- no sunlit treatment
- no open modern library plan
- no public-reading-room furniture set

## LABELS TO INCLUDE
- `shelves`
- `lane`
- `stairwell`
- `heavy door`
- `restricted route`

## UNCERTAINTY HANDLING
If a landmark or side room is uncertain:
- keep the shelving-city identity primary
- label the specific landmark reconstructed if needed
- do not flatten the district into a neat rectangle

## CONSISTENCY REQUIREMENTS
- Keep north aligned with the master campus map.
- Keep scale consistent with the approved building shell.
- Preserve previously approved entrances, corridors, stairs, and adjacent rooms.
- Do not contradict earlier approved plans.
- If a new detail would force a contradiction, reject the detail rather than improvising.
