Create a strictly top-down, orthographic architectural site plan of Hollows and its immediate exterior context from Patrick Rothfuss's *Kingkiller Chronicle*.

This is a canon-constrained reconstruction, not fantasy concept art.
Do not solve unresolved canon questions by invention.

## OUTPUT GOAL
Produce a single black-and-white site plan showing:
- the full exterior footprint of Hollows
- the short approach from the Archives side
- the face with colored or stained-glass windows
- the two big maples out front
- the main entrance and the known back entrance
- only enough surrounding ground to explain how the building sits near the Archives cluster

## DRAWING MODE
Use:
- true top-down orthographic view
- clean black-and-white architectural linework
- restrained scholarly presentation
- simple labels
- north arrow
- scale bar
- plain legend with `canon`, `implied`, and `reconstructed`

Do not use:
- perspective
- decorative fantasy borders
- cinematic light
- characters
- invented cloisters or ornamental gardens

## LOCKED CANON
- Hollows is simple and square.
- It has stained-glass windows depicting Teccam speaking at the mouth of his cave.
- From the Archives it is reached by going down and then left.
- It is identifiable from outside by its colored windows and two big maples out front.
- Hollows contains the admissions theatre.
- Hollows has at least one back entrance and locked internal routes.
- Elodin has an office in Hollows.

## STRONG IMPLICATIONS
- Hollows is a compact formal building rather than a sprawling complex.
- One exterior face should read as the more ceremonial or recognizable public face.
- The back entrance should feel practical and less public than the front approach.
- The plan must leave room for admissions traffic and controlled entry.

## OPENING AND MASSING DISCIPLINE
- Keep the footprint square and compact; do not sprout extra wings unless clearly labeled reconstructed.
- Put the stained-glass emphasis on the approach face or another clearly visible exterior face.
- Place the two maples on the front approach side, not randomly elsewhere on the plot.
- Show one main public entrance and one back entrance only.
- If you imply Elodin's lecture hall within Hollows, label that relationship `reconstructed` or `not frozen canon`; canon locks the lecture hall form, not the building assignment.
- Keep exterior windows limited to the stained-glass logic and other conservative openings that suit a formal academic building.

## HARD BANS
- no sprawling annexes treated as canon
- no ornate cathedral exterior
- no single-entry-only interpretation
- no unlabelled lecture-hall placement as settled canon
- no disconnection from the Archives-side approach

## LABELS TO INCLUDE
- `Hollows`
- `main approach`
- `main entrance`
- `back entrance`
- `maples`
- `stained-glass face`
- reconstructed labels if any lecture-hall or annex logic is shown

## UNCERTAINTY HANDLING
If an office cluster, secondary door, or side court is uncertain:
- keep it minimal
- mark it reconstructed
- do not multiply entrances or wings just to make the building feel complete

## CONSISTENCY REQUIREMENTS
- Keep north aligned with the master campus map.
- Keep scale consistent with the approved building shell.
- Preserve previously approved entrances, corridors, stairs, and adjacent rooms.
- Do not contradict earlier approved plans.
- If a new detail would force a contradiction, reject the detail rather than improvising.
