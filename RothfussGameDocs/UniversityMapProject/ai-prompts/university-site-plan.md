## LOCKED CAMPUS ANCHORS
The plan must preserve these high-confidence anchors:
- the University lies **west of Imre**
- the eastern chokepoint is **Stonebridge** over the Omethi
- the **Archives** are the strongest exterior anchor on the University side: a **five-story**, square, grey, windowless monolith visible from the bridge approach
- **Mains** is the sprawling, accreted, chaotic academic core rather than a neat block
- **Hollows** is a simple square stained-glass building near the Archives and is reached from the Archives by going **down, then left**
- **Medica** is a large irregular institutional building encountered opposite or beyond the Archives from one known approach
- the **Fishery / Artificery** is an industrial working complex, not a ceremonial building
- the **Mews** are a radial residential complex with a central hub and eight wings
- the **Mess** lies across open lawn to the south of the Mews
- **Haven** sits outside the dense student core to the north as a fenced, road-approached secure compound

## WHAT THE CAMPUS SHOULD FEEL LIKE
The University should read as:
- a major academic complex serving over a thousand students
- embedded in a town-sized, orderly service environment
- spatially varied rather than uniformly monumental
- shaped by roads, paths, lawns, courts, gardens, and working yards
- neither a tiny cloister nor a palace-campus
