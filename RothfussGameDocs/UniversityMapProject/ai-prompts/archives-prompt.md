# AI Image Generation Prompt: The Archives - Building Overview Pack

Use this as a building-wide visual pack after the site plan and floor plan are frozen. This is not the authoritative floor-by-floor prompt; it is the overall identity sheet for the Archives.

## Base Style Guidelines
- medieval academic stone architecture
- severe monolithic exterior
- controlled artificial light inside
- technical clarity over fantasy flourish
- dust, dryness, silence, and hierarchy

## Building Overview Prompt

Generate a canon-constrained visual overview of The Archives from Patrick Rothfuss's Kingkiller Chronicle.

Locked canon:
- Five-story square grey stone monolith.
- No windows and no exterior ornament.
- One great public entrance that is not obvious from the approach.
- Two-stage threshold: stone doors, small antechamber, then interior.
- Branching threshold with `TOMES`, `STACKS`, and `SCRIVS ONLY`.
- `Tomes` is the public, better-lit reading room.
- The deeper `Stacks` are dark, maze-like, and navigated with hand lamps.
- The interior behaves like a city of roads, lanes, alleys, shortcuts, stairwells, and named districts.
- Deep Archives areas include `Sub-three`, shelving coordinates, and Puppet's chambers.
- The roof is bare stone and reached by locked interior door.

Hard bans:
- no windows
- no extra entrances
- no decorative towers
- no bright modern library lighting
- no neat single-room interpretation of the building

## Scene Set

### Exterior
- blank grey five-story monolith
- one hard-to-find entrance
- restrained institutional surroundings

### Threshold
- compact antechamber
- desk and permissions logic
- three branching doors

### Tomes
- formal public reading room
- long tables
- better light than the rest of the building

### Stacks
- dark shelving maze
- hand-lamp scale lighting
- district-like circulation

### Deep Archives
- named lower routes such as `Sub-three`
- inhabited anomaly of Puppet's chambers
- sense of vast hidden order beneath the public face

### Roof
- bare stone rooftop
- parapet logic only if conservative
- locked roof access from within

Consistency requirements:
- Keep north aligned with the master campus map.
- Keep scale consistent with the approved building shell.
- Preserve previously approved entrances, corridors, stairs, and adjacent rooms.
- Do not contradict earlier approved plans.
