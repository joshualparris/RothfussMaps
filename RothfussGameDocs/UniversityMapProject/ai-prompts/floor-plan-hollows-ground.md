Create a strictly top-down floor plan for Hollows, Ground Floor, from Patrick Rothfuss's *Kingkiller Chronicle* as a canon-constrained reconstruction.

## FLOOR IDENTITY
This floor should read as a compact square formal building with admissions logic, office logic, and controlled circulation rather than a generic school corridor block.

## LOCKED CANON
- The building shell is simple and square.
- Hollows contains the admissions theatre.
- The admissions room is dark and theatre-like with a stage and a large raised crescent table for the masters.
- Hollows has a back entrance and lockable internal routes.
- Elodin has an office in Hollows.
- The building is identifiable from outside by stained-glass windows and two maples.

## STRONG IMPLICATIONS
- The admissions theatre should dominate one side or end of the plan.
- The back entrance should feed a more private corridor than the public face.
- One or more office rooms should exist without turning the whole building into offices.
- If Elodin's lecture hall is included in this floor, that is a reconstructed project bridge, not frozen canon.

## PLACEMENT DISCIPLINE
- Keep the outer walls square and the inner plan compact.
- Place the admissions theatre as the primary room with a clear threshold from the more public side of the building.
- Put the raised masters' table on the stage end of the theatre, not floating at the center.
- Place the back entrance on a secondary exterior wall and connect it to a lockable corridor or office route.
- Put Elodin's office on a quieter side corridor rather than directly inside the theatre.
- If a lecture-hall zone is shown, keep it explicitly labeled `reconstructed` or `not frozen canon` and do not let it erase the admissions-theatre hierarchy.
- Exterior windows belong only on perimeter walls; the stained-glass side should be identifiable but not overdecorated.

## FIXED ELEMENTS AND FURNITURE
- The admissions theatre should include the raised crescent masters' table as a fixed dominant element.
- Candidate or audience space may be sparse and conservative; do not fill the room with dense invented seating unless labeled reconstructed.
- Office rooms may include desk, chair, and shelving, but keep furnishings minimal and academic.

## HARD BANS
- no non-square master plan
- no single undivided hall with no back-entry logic
- no unlabelled lecture-hall placement as canon
