Create a strictly top-down, orthographic architectural site plan of the Medica and its immediate exterior context from Patrick Rothfuss's *Kingkiller Chronicle*.

This is a canon-constrained reconstruction, not fantasy concept art.

## OUTPUT GOAL
Produce a single black-and-white site plan showing:
- the full exterior massing of the Medica
- only the immediate paths, yard edges, or adjoining academic context needed to orient the building
- the approach side that makes sense relative to the Archives-side clue
- enough exterior logic to support treatment rooms and recovery spaces inside

## DRAWING MODE
Use:
- true top-down orthographic view
- clean black-and-white architectural linework
- restrained academic presentation
- simple labels
- north arrow
- scale bar
- plain legend with `canon`, `implied`, and `reconstructed`

## LOCKED CANON
- The Medica is on the other side of the Archives from one known approach.
- The Medica is a large, oddly shaped building.
- It is like a taller and less rambling version of Mains.
- It treats not only students but also sick people from the town, and some debt can be worked off after recovery.

## STRONG IMPLICATIONS
- The Medica must read as a substantial institution, not a tiny surgery.
- The building should be more orderly than Mains without becoming symmetrical or palace-like.
- The site should support both ordinary campus traffic and more serious patient arrival.
- The exterior should plausibly allow perimeter windows for treatment and recovery rooms.

## OPENING AND MASSING DISCIPLINE
- Keep the mass irregular but coherent: bent wings, unequal projections, or a stepped footprint are acceptable.
- Place one main receiving entrance on the side most likely to face campus traffic.
- If you add a secondary service or ward exit, keep it minimal and label it reconstructed.
- Use exterior windows conservatively along perimeter walls; do not make the Medica blind like the Archives.
- Let one quieter edge or wing read as the likely recovery side, but do not invent a formal cloister garden unless explicitly labeled reconstructed.

## HARD BANS
- no tiny clinic footprint
- no decorative noble manor look
- no residence or dining-hall identity
- no perfectly rectangular block that ignores the explicit odd shape
- no disconnection from the Archives-side clue

## LABELS TO INCLUDE
- `the Medica`
- `main entrance`
- `approach path`
- `recovery side` if used
- `service` only if clearly reconstructed

## UNCERTAINTY HANDLING
If a wing or outbuilding is uncertain:
- keep it small
- label it reconstructed
- do not overpopulate the site with invented medical sub-buildings

## CONSISTENCY REQUIREMENTS
- Keep north aligned with the master campus map.
- Keep scale consistent with the approved building shell.
- Preserve previously approved entrances, corridors, stairs, and adjacent rooms.
- Do not contradict earlier approved plans.
- If a new detail would force a contradiction, reject the detail rather than improvising.
