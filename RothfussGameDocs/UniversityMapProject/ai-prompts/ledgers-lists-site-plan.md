Create a strictly top-down, orthographic architectural site plan of Ledgers and Lists and its immediate exterior context from Patrick Rothfuss's *Kingkiller Chronicle*.

This is a canon-constrained reconstruction, not fantasy concept art.

## OUTPUT GOAL
Produce a single black-and-white site plan showing:
- a modest administrative building or office suite footprint
- the exterior approach for student course-registration traffic
- only enough context to explain how a queueable public-facing records office works on campus

- Ledgers and Lists is a distinct administrative office or records room used for class sign-up.
