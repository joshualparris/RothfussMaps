Create a strictly top-down room plan for the admissions theatre in Hollows from Patrick Rothfuss's *Kingkiller Chronicle* as a canon-constrained reconstruction.

## ROOM IDENTITY
This room is a dark, authority-heavy ceremonial chamber for admissions, not a generic classroom.

## LOCKED CANON
- The room is theatre-like.
- It is dark.
- It has a stage.
- The masters sit behind a large raised crescent-shaped table.

## STRONG IMPLICATIONS
- The candidate or audience should face the raised crescent table.
- The room should feel controlled, formal, and intimidating.
- Entry should occur from the less important end or side of the room, not through the masters' stage.

## ROOM ENVELOPE AND PLACEMENT DISCIPLINE
- Use one main doorway from a corridor or vestibule and place it away from the raised stage.
- Put the stage and crescent table on the dominant end of the room.
- Keep the room either rectangular or gently fan-shaped, but do not turn it into a giant public theatre.
- If windows are shown at all, keep them limited and subordinate to the room's darkness; do not flood the theatre with bright exterior light.
- Keep wall lines simple and institutional.

## FIXED ELEMENTS AND FURNITURE
- The raised crescent table is mandatory and should dominate the plan.
- Audience seating, benches, or standing room may be shown conservatively, but do not invent a packed auditorium unless labeled reconstructed.
- Keep all secondary furniture sparse.

## LIGHT AND MATERIALS
- Light should concentrate around the masters' end rather than uniformly across the room.
- Materials should read as stone, wood, and practical academic finish rather than ornamented fantasy carving.

## HARD BANS
- no domestic furniture
- no bright cheerful lecture room lighting
- no unraised masters' table
- no dense theatre seating treated as canon

## LABELS TO INCLUDE
- `entry`
- `stage`
- `raised crescent table`
- `candidate floor` or `audience space`

## UNCERTAINTY HANDLING
If bench count or exact room proportions are uncertain:
- keep the seating sparse
- preserve the stage hierarchy
- prefer blank floor space over invented detail

## CONSISTENCY REQUIREMENTS
- Keep north aligned with the master campus map.
- Keep scale consistent with the approved building shell.
- Preserve previously approved entrances, corridors, stairs, and adjacent rooms.
- Do not contradict earlier approved plans.
- If a new detail would force a contradiction, reject the detail rather than improvising.
