Create a strictly top-down floor plan for Ledgers and Lists, Ground Floor, from Patrick Rothfuss's *Kingkiller Chronicle* as a canon-constrained reconstruction.

## FLOOR IDENTITY
This should feel like a compact but busy student-facing records office where course sign-up happens, not like a giant archive or ceremonial hall.

## LOCKED CANON
- Ledgers and Lists is the University office used to sign up for classes.

## STRONG IMPLICATIONS
- There should be a public-facing transaction area.
- Clerical desks, ledgers, and records storage are central to the room's identity.
- The office likely separates student access from deeper records handling.

## PLACEMENT DISCIPLINE
- Put one clear public entrance on the approach side.
- Place a waiting or queue zone immediately inside the entrance.
- Use a counter, desk line, or barrier to separate students from clerical space.
- Put shelves, ledgers, cubbies, or cupboards along walls behind the counter.
- Add one back records room or staff room deeper inside rather than many invented departments.
- Exterior windows are allowed only on perimeter walls and should suit a modest office, not a dormitory.

## FIXED ELEMENTS AND FURNITURE
- Include a front counter or clerk desk as the dominant fixed feature.
- Include ledger shelves, writing surfaces, stools or chairs, and perhaps a standing rail for students filling out information.
- Keep furnishings orderly and clerical rather than decorative.

- no industrial workshop arrangement
- no dormitory or dining-hall furniture
- no many-room bureaucratic maze unsupported by evidence
