# Hollows - Building Brief

## Building Identity
Admissions and ceremonial building, square with stained glass, contains masters' offices.

## Confirmed Facts
- Simple and square, with stained glass windows depicting Teccam speaking at the mouth of his cave
- Contains admissions theatre: empty theatre-like room with raised crescent-shaped masters' table in dark space lit mainly around the table
- Has back entrance with locks, allowing internal partitioning and alternative access
- Elodin has an office in Hollows, though he never seems to use it- Contains Elodin's lecture hall: huge empty lecture hall entered from hallway above, students walk down stairs to front row, large slate at front- Reached from Archives by going "down, then left"; identifiable by colored windows and two big maples outside
- Masters' Hall may be accessed through Hollows via Jamison route

## Implied Facts
- Ceremonial and administrative functions with theatrical staging
- Multiple access points and circulation paths
- Contains at least one master's office space
- Positioned lower or farther down grade from Archives

## Explicit Design Inferences
- Square stone building with notable stained glass façade
- Interior theatre space designed to reinforce authority through elevation and controlled lighting
- Complex access patterns with public and private routes
- Administrative offices for faculty members

## Exterior Shell
Square stone building with stained glass windows and twin maple trees outside.

## Entrances and Circulation
- Main entrance from Archives approach ("down, then left")
- Back entrance with lockable access
- Internal routes to admissions theatre and offices
- Possible connection to Masters' Hall

## Required Functions
- Admissions ceremonies with theatrical staging
- Master's offices (at least Elodin's)
- Access route to Masters' Hall
- Stained glass as identifying landmark
