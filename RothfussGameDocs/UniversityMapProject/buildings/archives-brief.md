# The Archives - Building Brief

## Building Identity
The University's monolithic knowledge fortress: public at the threshold, labyrinthine and heavily tiered within.

## Locked Canon
- Five stories tall, square, grey, featureless, windowless, and visually dominant from Stonebridge.
- One great stone entrance, not placed on the obvious approach side.
- Public entry sequence is stone doors -> small antechamber -> inner doors.
- The antechamber is an active gatekeeping desk, not a passive foyer.
- From the threshold, the building branches to `TOMES`, `STACKS`, and `SCRIVS ONLY`.
- `Tomes` is a large public reading room with good light and long tables.
- The deeper `Stacks` are dark, dry, cool, and lamp-dependent.
- Access is ledger-controlled; being "in the book" matters.
- `Tomes only` is a lower permission tier than deeper Archives access.
- The interior behaves like a city, with roads, alleys, lanes, shortcuts, and named districts.
- Named work zones include Scriptorium, Sorting Hall, Buggery, Cataloger's Mew, Bindery, Bolts, Palimpsest, and the acquisitions office.
- The acquisitions office is tiny, dark, and dominated by a large operational map.
- The four-plate door is a deep internal landmark.
- At least one southeast stair anchor is named inside the Stacks.
- `Sub-three` proves there are named deep levels below ordinary routes.
- Puppet's chambers prove there is at least one inhabited room in the belly of the Archives.
- Shelving can be addressed by floor, corner, row, rack, shelf, and side.
- The roof is bare stone, reached by locked interior door, and overlooks a courtyard.
- The building connects to the Underthing through service or ventilation routes.

## Frozen Design Decisions
- Treat the ground-floor public face as compact and controlled, with the true scale hidden behind threshold layers.
- Treat `Sub-three` as below the ordinary Stacks circulation rather than part of public reading space.
- Keep Puppet deep inside the secure maze, not as a quirky room near the entrance.
- Use the shelving coordinate system as proof of internal rigor inside an otherwise bewildering city-like plan.

## Major Zones
- Threshold: stone entry, desk, branching doors, access control.
- Public: `Tomes`.
- Restricted: `Stacks`, stair cores, deeper districts, four-plate door.
- Staff or process spaces: Scriptorium, Sorting Hall, Buggery, acquisitions, bindery-type work areas.
- Hidden or deep: `Sub-three`, Puppet's chambers, Underthing-linked service routes, roof access stair.

## Hard Bans
- No exterior windows.
- No exterior ornament.
- No multiple public entrances.
- No evenly daylit Stacks.
- No tidy modern library grid replacing the internal city logic.
- No open-flame atmosphere throughout general Stacks circulation.

## Prompt-Ready Summary
Five-story square grey windowless stone monolith with one hard-to-find public entrance and a two-stage threshold. Ground level should read as access control and public reading, while the deeper building opens into a book-city of dark storage districts, workrooms, stair anchors, deep named levels, and hidden inhabited pockets. Keep the exterior severe and the interior stratified, navigable, and intimidating rather than neat.
