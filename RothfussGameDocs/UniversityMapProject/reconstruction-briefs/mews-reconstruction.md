# The Mews - Canon-Constrained Reconstruction Brief

## 1. What is explicitly known
- Circular central hub with eight wings radiating outward like a compass rose.
- Grey, many-windowed, and externally described as three stories tall.
- Ground-floor bathing facilities exist.
- Non-Arcanum bunks are on the fourth floor of the east wing, farthest from the baths.
- Arcanum students are housed in the west wing.
- The Mess is across lawn to the south.

## 2. What is strongly implied
- Radial circulation centered on the hub.
- Wing-based residential assignment by status.
- Vertical circulation matters because room placement is socially meaningful.
- The building is a proper dormitory complex, not a simple hall with bunks.

## 3. What is weakly inferred
- Some wings may differ slightly in height or roof treatment.
- The uppermost east-wing accommodation may be attic-like, split-level, or otherwise less obvious from outside.
- The hub likely contains the main stairs and orientation space.

## 4. What must not be contradicted
- Do not flatten the Mews into a rectangle.
- Do not ignore the three-story versus fourth-floor tension.
- Do not move baths off the ground floor.
- Do not swap the known east-wing and west-wing roles.

## 5. Likely building function and circulation
The Mews functions as a radial residential complex whose plan helps sort and separate student housing. Movement likely centers on the hub and stairs, while wing assignment and floor distance reinforce status and convenience differences.

## 6. Likely entrances, stairs, and room zones
- Main approach from the campus side, with south relationship toward the Mess across lawn.
- Central hub.
- Eight radiating wings.
- Ground-floor communal baths.
- Residential upper floors.
- Reconstructed partial upper east-wing level to reconcile the fourth-floor bunk reference.

## 7. Unknowns that must remain unknown
- Exact wing dimensions.
- Exact room count.
- Exact stair count.
- Whether the partial upper level exists only in the east wing or more broadly.

## 8. Final reconstruction summary
The Mews is a radial dormitory centered on a circular hub with eight wings, planned for status-based student housing and daily circulation around shared baths. It should read externally as a three-story, many-windowed residence while still supporting a reconstructed partial upper east-wing level that preserves the canon fourth-floor reference.
