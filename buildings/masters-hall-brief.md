# Masters' Hall - Building Brief

## Building Identity
Residential and administrative building for University masters, grey stone with stained glass.

## Confirmed Facts
- Grey stone construction
- At night, dim interior light shows stained glass of Teccam speaking at the mouth of his cave
- Contains living quarters for the masters, resident gillers, and guest rooms for visiting arcanists
- Large windowless room with familiar crescent-shaped table (not elevated), reached via Hollows/Jamison route
- Accessed roughly toward Hollows from the Mess

## Implied Facts
- Primarily residential and administrative rather than teaching-focused
- Connected to or adjacent to Hollows
- Contains formal meeting spaces with crescent table arrangement
- May share stained glass motif with Hollows

## Explicit Design Inferences
- Formal academic residential building
- Windowless meeting room for confidential discussions
- Crescent table as recurring motif in University governance spaces
- Guest accommodations for visiting scholars

## Exterior Shell
Grey stone building with stained glass windows visible at night.

## Entrances and Circulation
- Accessed via Hollows/Jamison route from Mess area
- Internal circulation between residential quarters and meeting spaces
- Possible connection to Hollows building

## Required Functions
- Masters' living quarters
- Gillers' residences
- Guest rooms for visiting arcanists
- Formal meeting room with crescent table
- Administrative and governance spaces
