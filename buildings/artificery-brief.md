# The Artificery - Building Brief

## Building Identity
Workshop for metalwork and artificing, containing heat and tools.

## Confirmed Facts
- Hot environment with metalwork tang
- Hammering from deeper within
- Connected to Fishery workspace
- Kilvin oversees operations
- Kilvin's office opens off the workshop with small forge/glasswork in corner, hot from stoked fire, heavy door
- Southern exit opens toward courtyard
- Eastern wall has low windows allowing morning sunlight to slant through
- Workshop feels hollow and lifeless early in morning with few students

## Implied Facts
- Industrial workspace with forges and workbenches
- Ventilation for heat and fumes
- Storage for materials and tools

## Explicit Design Inferences
- Single-story workshop with high ceilings for heat dissipation
- Stone construction with metal reinforcements
- Separate areas for different processes

## Exterior Shell
Rectangular stone workshop, 35x25 meters, single story with high roof.

## Entrances and Circulation
- North entrance from Mains
- Internal connection to Fishery
- Work areas with clear pathways

## Required Room Functions
- Main workshop floor
- Forge areas
- Tool storage
- Connected Fishery space

## Hard Bans
- No residential or lecture functions
- No delicate materials (books, etc.)

## Open Questions
- Exact layout of work stations

## Final Frozen Brief
Single-story stone workshop with high ceilings for metalwork and artificing. Hot environment with industrial equipment. Connected to Fishery workspace. Medieval craft guild architecture.
