# AI Image Generation Prompt: The Mews - Floor Plan

Use this prompt with Gemini/Nano Banana for generating floor plans. Generate the entire floor first, then individual rooms.

## Base Style Guidelines (include in all prompts):
- Medieval university architecture: stone walls, wooden beams, arched doorways
- Atmospheric lighting: natural light through windows, lanterns, dust motes
- Canon-consistent: worn stone floors, academic atmosphere, constant activity
- Technical drawing style: clear labels, scale indicators, north arrow
- Color palette: earth tones, aged materials, subtle lighting

## Floor Plan Generation Prompt:

Generate a detailed architectural floor plan for The Mews, the radial student dormitory of the University from Patrick Rothfuss's Kingkiller Chronicle.

Building specifications:
- Circular central hub with eight wings radiating outward like a compass rose
- Three stories tall, grey stone, many-windowed building
- South entrance from the courtyard
- East wing: non-Arcanum students, with bunks on fourth floor farthest from ground baths
- West wing: Arcanum students
- Ground floor: bathing facilities
- Mess across lawn to the south

Key features to include:
- Circular central hub as circulation center
- Eight radiating wings (compass directions)
- Vertical circulation between floors
- Ground floor communal bathing
- Status-based room assignments by wing
- Multiple windows throughout

Output: Clean architectural floor plan with:
- Clear room boundaries and labels
- Door and window symbols
- Scale reference
- North arrow pointing up
- Legend explaining symbols
- Multiple floor levels shown

## Individual Room Prompts (generate after floor plan):

### Central Hub:
Generate an interior view of the central hub in The Mews.

Key elements:
- Circular stone room at building center
- Eight corridors radiating outward like compass points
- Central staircase to upper floors
- Stone walls with arched openings
- Functional circulation space
- Natural light from windows

Style: Atmospheric medieval hub, circular stone architecture, focus on radial symmetry.

### East Wing Corridor:
Generate an interior view of an east wing corridor in The Mews.

Key elements:
- Narrow corridor with thin wooden doors to rooms
- Stone walls, wooden floorboards
- Small windows letting in light
- Doors to student rooms
- Simple, functional dormitory hallway

Style: Atmospheric medieval dormitory, practical and lived-in.

### Student Room:
Generate an interior view of a typical student room in The Mews.

Key elements:
- Small room with bed, chair, minimal furnishings
- Simple wooden bed with blankets
- Small desk or chair
- Window for light
- Sparse belongings
- Clean but basic accommodations

Style: Atmospheric medieval student housing, minimal and functional.

### Bathing Facilities:
Generate an interior view of the ground floor bathing facilities.

Key elements:
- Communal bathing area with tubs or basins
- Stone floors, functional plumbing
- Towels and bathing supplies
- Steam or water vapor in air
- Practical, no-frills facilities
- Access from central hub

Style: Atmospheric medieval bathhouse, functional and utilitarian.

Consistency requirements:
- Keep north aligned with the master campus map.
- Keep scale consistent with the approved building shell.
- Preserve previously approved entrances, corridors, stairs, and adjacent rooms.
- Do not contradict earlier approved plans.
- If a new detail would force a contradiction, reject the detail rather than improvising.
