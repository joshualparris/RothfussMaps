# AI Image Generation Prompt: The Mains - Ground Floor Plan

Use this prompt with Gemini/Nano Banana for generating floor plans. Generate the entire floor first, then individual rooms.

## Base Style Guidelines (include in all prompts):
- Medieval university architecture: stone walls, wooden beams, arched doorways
- Atmospheric lighting: natural light through tall windows, lanterns, dust motes
- Canon-consistent: worn stone floors, academic atmosphere, constant activity
- Technical drawing style: clear labels, scale indicators, north arrow
- Color palette: earth tones, aged materials, subtle lighting

## Floor Plan Generation Prompt:

Generate a detailed architectural floor plan for the ground floor of The Mains, the central hub building of the University from Patrick Rothfuss's Kingkiller Chronicle.

Building specifications:
- Rectangular stone building, approximately 50x30 meters
- Four main entrances: north (to Artificery), east (to Archives), south (to Medica), west (to Courtyard)
- Central hall for circulation
- Central staircase to upper floor
- Medieval academic architecture with stone walls and wooden accents

Key features to include:
- Main hall as central circulation space with worn stone floors
- Four directional exits clearly marked
- Central staircase location
- Natural lighting through windows
- Functional, utilitarian design without decorative excess

Output: Clean architectural floor plan with:
- Clear room boundaries and labels
- Door and window symbols
- Scale reference
- North arrow pointing up
- Legend explaining symbols

## Individual Room Prompts (generate after floor plan):

### Main Hall Interior:
Generate an interior view of the Main Hall in The Mains, showing the central circulation space.

Key elements:
- Worn stone floors with foot traffic patterns
- Stone walls with arched doorways to four directions
- Wooden beams and supports
- Natural light filtering through high windows
- Constant activity: students passing, voices murmuring
- Dust motes in beams of light
- Academic atmosphere: books, cloaks, purposeful movement

Style: Atmospheric interior, medieval university, detailed but not cluttered, focus on space and flow.

Consistency requirements:
- Keep north aligned with the master campus map.
- Keep scale consistent with the approved building shell.
- Preserve previously approved entrances, corridors, stairs, and adjacent rooms.
- Do not contradict earlier approved plans.
- If a new detail would force a contradiction, reject the detail rather than improvising.
