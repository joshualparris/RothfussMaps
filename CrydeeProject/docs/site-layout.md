# Crydee Site Layout Draft

Status: working draft, not frozen

## Working Scope

This document covers Castle Crydee and its immediate settlement context, not the full Duchy of Crydee.

## Strongest Current Anchors

- Castle Crydee contains a keep.
- The town sits below the keep or castle level, not as one flat merged zone.
- The great hall is a central noble-public room where the Duke holds court.
- The site includes a courtyard and a great-gate sequence with a smaller postern.
- A herald balcony overlooks the courtyard.
- The keep faces the courtyard with broad stairs and large doors.
- The site includes walls, towers, battlements, and specifically named west and east defensive edges.
- The stable side includes a wall used as a recognizable access edge.
- A kitchen-to-great-hall corridor exists.
- The keep includes a council chamber and nearby private quarters.
- The castle includes barracks, an armory, cellar storage, and a northmost magician's tower tied to the kitchen route.
- The Princess's small flower garden sits on the side of the keep behind screening hedges.

## Working Spatial Logic

- V03 walkthrough orientation note:
  the playable coordinate frame uses north at higher `z`, south at lower `z`, west at lower `x`, and east at higher `x`.
- In that V03 frame, the great gate faces the road, town-below, and sea-view approach at `z > 72`; the east clearing and forest sit beyond `x > 84`; the west assault field sits beyond `x < 0`.
- This orientation is a practical reconstruction for the walkthrough and should not be mistaken for a final canonical map projection.
- The approach runs from the great gate into a gatehouse passage and then into the main courtyard.
- The gate towers and herald balcony sit above or alongside that approach sequence.
- The courtyard is the outdoor gathering space where formal ceremonies and everyday traffic meet.
- The keep front should face the courtyard with a broad stair landing before the main hall doors.
- The great hall sits as the public heart of the keep, with more private ducal rooms behind it.
- The west side of the site reads as a service and garrison edge with stables, barracks, and armory.
- The east side of the site reads as the keep's service spine: storage, corridor, kitchen, pantry, cellar access, and magician's tower.
- The Princess's garden belongs on the side of the keep rather than in the center of the court.
- West wall and east wall references should remain meaningful orientation anchors for future siege-state variants.

## Things We Should Not Pretend To Know Yet

- exact full shell geometry beyond the currently justified blockout
- exact tower count
- exact wall thicknesses
- exact gate count beyond the great gate and its smaller door
- the precise relationship between cliff edge, sea approach, and settlement streets
- whether later evidence requires splitting the current baseline from a dedicated siege-state target

## Working Reconstruction Bridges

- Use one baseline Castle Crydee layout for peacetime life unless a scene explicitly requires siege-state differences.
- Treat the current anchor chain as:
  town below -> great gate -> gatehouse passage -> courtyard -> keep stairs -> great hall -> inner hall -> ducal rooms.
- Treat the west-side yard, stables, barracks, and armory as one service-garrison wing.
- Treat the east-side storage, corridor, kitchen, cellar access, and magician's tower as one service-magic wing.
- Allow a restrained cellar layer and restrained upper gatehouse and tower layers for playable verification, but keep them explicitly reconstructed.

## Freeze Criteria

Do not freeze this layout until:

- the evidence ledger has a broader Crydee pass
- directional and adjacency clues have been cross-checked
- obvious fantasy-castle assumptions have been stripped out
- the expanded v2 blockout has been walked and sanity-checked against the quoted scenes
