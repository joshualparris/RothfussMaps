# Crydee Evidence Summary v1

This started as a seed summary and now reflects the first broader spatial pass. It is still not a complete line-by-line extraction of the novel.

## Current High-Value Spatial Anchors

- `pdf p. 11`: Castle Crydee is explicitly named, and it contains a keep.
- `pdf p. 12`: Castle walls and towers are established strongly enough to matter for exterior shell planning, and the kitchen stores are kept in the cellars.
- `pdf p. 19-20`: The Choosing occurs in the courtyard, confirming that the court is a real ceremonial gathering space.
- `pdf p. 20`: A herald balcony overlooks the courtyard, the great gate contains a smaller door, and broad stairs rise to large keep doors.
- `pdf p. 25`: Activity is described across the keep and the town below, supporting a vertical or elevated castle-settlement relationship; the guard barracks are also named.
- `pdf p. 26`: Pug's room is in the northmost magician's tower and provides a shortcut through the kitchen; giant barrels are hauled from the cellars into the courtyard.
- `pdf p. 43`: A gate tower lookout spots returning riders and triggers a guard response.
- `pdf p. 50`: An armory exists as an active castle room.
- `pdf p. 53`, `p. 57`, `p. 70`: The Duke repeatedly convenes people in the council chamber.
- `pdf p. 55-56`: The Duke's private quarters sit off the keep circulation, and the Princess's small flower garden lies on the side of the keep up three steps with a bench and screened view of the courtyard.
- `pdf p. 159`: A long corridor connects the kitchen and the great hall.
- `pdf p. 229`: The cellars also function as a refuge during siege conditions.
- `pdf p. 241-251`: The west wall, east wall, battlements, and the broad clearing along the eastern wall all matter during the siege.

## What This Tells Us Now

- Crydee should be reconstructed as a compact multi-zone fortress, not a single hall with decorative outbuildings.
- The novel supports a real gate approach, a real court, a real keep front, and a real split between noble rooms, service spaces, and garrison spaces.
- The kitchen side of the castle carries more spatial weight than the first prototype showed because it anchors the corridor, the cellars, and the magician's tower route.
- The castle now has enough grounded evidence to justify a broader walkable v2 layout with cellar and upper-access areas, as long as those layers stay marked reconstructed where dimensions are guessed.

## What Still Needs Broader Review

- exact outer footprint and cliff relationship
- tower count beyond the named gate tower and magician's tower
- whether the herald balcony belongs to the gatehouse, the keep facade, or a combined upper approach layer
- how much of the battlement circulation should become explicit playable wall-walk geometry
