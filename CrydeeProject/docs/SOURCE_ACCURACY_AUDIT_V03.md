# Castle Crydee Source-Accuracy Audit V03

Status: generated after the V03 source-accuracy pass

This audit describes what improved in the V03 Crydee walkthrough target, what is still uncertain, and where the reconstruction is still leaning on practical geometry rather than locked canon. It is not a claim that Crydee is now "final." It is a record of what the V03 pass did well and what still needs review.

## What V03 Improved

- The walkthrough now starts outside the great gate so the player experiences town-below, road approach, gate passage, courtyard, keep front, and great hall in the right order.
- The site no longer reads as an isolated slab. V03 adds a raised castle platform, approach road, town-below context, sea-view horizon, east clearing, forest edge, and west assault-field context.
- The generator now creates clearer defensive architecture: perimeter wall-walks, battlement markers, merlons, west-wall emphasis, east-clearing viewpoint, and alarm-bell context.
- The keep front is much easier to read. Broad stairs, large oaken doors, and the herald balcony now exist as scene elements rather than only labels.
- Great hall, service corridor, kitchen, cellar, and tower routes are staged with practical props so the castle reads more like a lived frontier keep and less like a diagram.
- The vertical links are longer and gentler than V02, which should make cellar, gatehouse, and tower traversal substantially more usable.
- The magician's tower now has a clearer Pug room and an upper Kulgan room relationship, even though the exact tower stack remains reconstructed.
- A certainty legend and certainty-coloured route markers make the canon / implied / reconstructed split much more visible in the walkthrough itself.

## What Is Still Canon

- Castle Crydee as a frontier ducal stronghold with a keep, great hall, courtyard, gate sequence, walls, towers, battlements, cellars, stables, kitchen-service route, and magician's tower.
- Town-below logic, gate closure logic, and the importance of the west and east defensive edges.
- Great gate plus smaller inset door.
- Broad keep-stair and oaken-door sequence into the great hall.
- Great hall public function.
- Great hall to kitchen service-corridor logic.
- Kitchen to cellar and tower access logic.
- Princess's garden as a side garden with screening character.
- Battlements and wall-fighting function.

## What Is Still Implied

- Exact shaping of some upper routes such as the gatehouse watch line and tower-to-upper-tower relationship.
- The exact placement and size of the private hall behind the great hall.
- The exact form of the alarm-bell location and some watch-specific markers.
- The exact stair runs used to make playable upper circulation.

## What Is Still Reconstructed

- The complete site topography, including exact grade changes between castle, town, and coast.
- The exact outer shell geometry, tower count, tower heights, and shell symmetry.
- Exact room dimensions and most wall thicknesses.
- The exact geometry of battlement circulation and tower-room stacking.
- The detailed shape of the town and coast beyond the gate.
- Most prop placements, staging furniture, and the exact service layout of the kitchen and cellar.

## What The Book Still Does Not Let Us Know Exactly

- The full shell of Castle Crydee as an architecturally complete plan.
- The exact number and placement of every tower.
- The exact relationship between bluffs, coast, streets, and castle terraces.
- The exact dimensions of the great hall, courtyard, walls, and tower rooms.
- The exact internal subdivision of private quarters and service spaces.

The correct discipline is still:

`canon function where supported -> implied relationship where strong -> reconstructed geometry where needed for playability`

## Bottom Line

V03 is a meaningful source-accuracy upgrade over V02. It moves Crydee from "good evidence-aware blockout" toward "credible evidence-led site model" by fixing the missing approach context, strengthening vertical circulation, improving defensive readability, and making the hero routes clearer.

It is still not a final canon lock. The castle now has a better source-led shape and a much better playable sequence, but the exact architecture is still partly unknowable from the novel and should stay labelled that way.
