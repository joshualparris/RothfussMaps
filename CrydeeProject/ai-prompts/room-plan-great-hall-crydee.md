You are generating a room-level concept for the great hall in Castle Crydee from `Magician`.

## Hard Rules

- Treat the great hall as a confirmed court room and major public-noble chamber.
- Do not invent exact dimensions, exact window count, or ornamental luxury unsupported by evidence.

## Output Goal

- noble enough for the Duke to hold court
