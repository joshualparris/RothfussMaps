# Castle Crydee Brief

Status: working draft, not frozen

## Building Identity

Castle Crydee is a frontier noble stronghold where ceremony, defense, and day-to-day labor sit in the same compact shell. It should feel inhabited, strategic, and workmanlike rather than like a giant fantasy palace.

## Locked Canon

- Castle Crydee contains a keep.
- The site includes walls and towers.
- The great hall exists and serves as a court room for the Duke.
- The castle includes a courtyard gate sequence.
- The great gate includes a smaller door within it.
- A balcony overlooks the courtyard.
- The keep faces the courtyard with broad stairs and large doors.
- A kitchen-to-great-hall corridor exists.
- The keep includes a council chamber and private quarters beyond the hall sequence.
- The castle includes guard barracks and an armory.
- The castle includes kitchen-connected cellars.
- The Princess has a small flower garden on the side of the keep.
- Pug's room is in the northmost magician's tower and uses a kitchen-side shortcut.
- The castle has a west wall with battlements.
- The castle also has a named east wall with a broad clearing along it.
- The town below is meaningfully distinct from the keep or castle level.

## Strong Implication

- The castle should read as a border fortress with noble, defensive, and service functions mixed together.
- The stable side belongs to a service and garrison edge rather than a ceremonial front.
- The courtyard is a practical circulation and gathering space that can also host formal events.
- The keep should sit deeper in the castle than the gate, with the courtyard mediating the approach.
- The east service spine should tie kitchen, pantry, cellar access, and the magician's tower together.

## Frozen Design Decisions

These are working bridges only. They are not final canon:

- Use one baseline Crydee state for general reconstruction and split siege-state versions only if later evidence forces it.
- Prioritize circulation anchors before decorative room inventory.
- Treat the current blockout target as castle-grounds-first with one restrained cellar layer and two restrained upper-access areas.
- Keep the first playable tower implementation to the magician's tower and gatehouse watch instead of inventing a full multi-tower network.

## Major Zones

- keep
- keep stair landing
- great hall
- inner hall and ducal chambers
- courtyard
- great gate and postern
- gate towers and upper watch
- wall-and-tower defensive shell
- stable-side service and garrison edge
- kitchen and service corridor network
- cellar storage and refuge layer
- Princess's side garden
- magician's tower
- settlement or town below

## Hard Bans

- No concentric mega-castle invention.
- No exact tower count without evidence.
- No inflated fantasy-palace styling.
- No precise dimensional claims presented as canon.
- No flattening town-below and castle into one level plane.

## Open Uncertainties

- exact footprint
- exact relationship of east and west battlements to the inner keep massing
- shoreline and cliff geometry
- stable placement precision
- number of towers beyond the named gate tower and magician's tower
- whether the west wall implies the main defensive pressure side in the baseline layout
- how much of the upper circulation should be represented as wall walks versus enclosed tower rooms

## Prompt-Ready Summary

Castle Crydee should be reconstructed as a practical frontier stronghold with a great gate and postern, a central courtyard, a keep fronted by broad stairs and large doors, a great hall with deeper ducal rooms, a west-side garrison and stable edge, an east-side kitchen and cellar spine, a side garden for the Princess, and a magician's tower tied to the kitchen route. Where the novel is vague, keep the layout restrained and clearly marked as reconstructed.
