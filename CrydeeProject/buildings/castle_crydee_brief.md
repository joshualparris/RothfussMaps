# Castle Crydee Building Brief

## Overview
Castle Crydee is a frontier outpost in the Kingdom of the Isles, serving as the seat of the Duke of Crydee. It's a medieval-style fortress with defensive walls, towers, and various functional buildings within.

## Canon Elements (Direct Book Descriptions)

### Exterior Features
- **Walls**: Stone walls surround the castle, with gates that close at night
- **Gates**: Main entrance gates, closed for the night
- **Stables**: Located near the walls, accessible from outside
- **Courtyard**: Central open area within the walls
- **Tower**: Magician Kulgan's tower for study and magic work

### Interior Spaces
- **Keep**: Main residential building, contains living quarters
- **Great Hall**: Likely the main dining/feasting area (implied from context)
- **Kitchen**: Food preparation area, overseen by cook Megar
- **Dining Hall**: Where meals are served
- **Barracks**: Soldier housing (implied from guards and soldiers)
- **Armory**: Weapon storage (implied from military function)

### Key Locations Mentioned
- **Tower of Testing**: Associated with magic training
- **Hearth/Fireplace**: In living areas for warmth
- **Tables/Chairs**: Furniture in dining and living areas
- **Beds**: Sleeping quarters
- **Windows**: Present in some rooms
- **Doors**: Wooden doors throughout

- No anachronistic features (electricity, modern plumbing)
- No fantasy elements beyond what's canon (elves, magic are separate)
- No excessive ornamentation - this is a frontier outpost, not a royal palace
- No underground complexes beyond basic cellars

## Layout Principles
- **Defensive Priority**: Walls, gates, and towers are primary features
- **Functional Design**: Spaces serve practical military and domestic needs
- **Medieval Authenticity**: Stone construction, wooden accents, practical layout
- **Scale**: Frontier castle, not massive like capital fortresses
