# Castle Crydee Reconstruction Brief

## 1. Reconstruction Goal

Reconstruct Castle Crydee as a grounded, evidence-led frontier stronghold from `Magician`, preserving uncertainty where the text is thin and using explicit reconstruction bridges only where needed for usability.

## 2. Core Identity

Crydee should feel like a defended noble seat at the edge of a harsher world. It must balance court life, daily castle labor, and credible defensive logic.

## 3. Confirmed Anchors

- a keep
- a great hall
- a courtyard gate sequence
- walls and towers
- a west wall with battlements
- a stable-side wall reference
- a direct service corridor between kitchen and great hall
- a town below distinct from the castle level

## 4. Strong Implications

- the castle should be compact enough to feel operational, not sprawling for spectacle alone
- service spaces matter because the narrative notices them
- the great hall is socially central
- the castle shell should feel practical and militarily meaningful

## 5. Reconstruction Bridges

- Use a restrained baseline site plan before attempting special siege versions.
- Treat west wall, courtyard gate, great hall, and town-below as first-pass anchors.
- Keep unverified towers, secondary courtyards, noble apartments, and shoreline geometry marked reconstructed or unknown.

## 6. First Blockout Priorities

- outer defensive shell
- keep anchor
- great hall anchor
- courtyard
- gate approach
- service-side wall and stable edge
- kitchen-to-great-hall circulation

## 7. Hard Bans

- do not overdesign
- do not turn uncertainty into polish
- do not let image aesthetics define layout
- do not import generic fantasy-castle symmetry

## 8. Recommended Next Step

Expand the evidence ledger substantially, then freeze a first Crydee site-layout pass before drafting `castle_crydee_site_v01.json`.
